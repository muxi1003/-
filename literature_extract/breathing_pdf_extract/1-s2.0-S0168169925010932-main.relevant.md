## Abstract/Summary Snippet

Respiratory Rate (RR) is a key physiological indicator in dairy cattle, providing insights into herd and individual health, as well as reactions to environmental factors like heat stress, thermo-resilience, and respiratory disease. Traditionally, RR is monitored by visually counting abdominal fluctuations, an approach that becomes impractical in large herds. This challenge creates an opportunity to implement Computer Vision (CV) methods, particularly deep learning networks designed for video assessment. In our study, we captured video recordings of Holstein cows during different milking sessions in the milking parlor. A total of 688 videos of the animals ’ dorsal view were manually annotated to count breaths over 30 s, identify suitable frames, and define the Region of Interest (ROI). The resulting dataset supported a comparative analysis between two architectures: R2Plus1D18 and MViTv2. We tested three ROI extraction techniques: Center Crop (CC), Full Abdomen Bounding Box (FABB), and One-Sided Portion of the Abdomen Bounding Box (OSPABB).
