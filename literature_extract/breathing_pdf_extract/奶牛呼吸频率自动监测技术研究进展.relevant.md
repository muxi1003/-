## Abstract/Summary Snippet

Driven by the proposition of “Internet + animal production” and the application of IOT in Agriculture, sensing and application of animal bio-information has been a popular research topic in precision livestock farming. Respiration rate (RR) of dairy cow is an important indicator to evaluate environmental comfort. With the development of automation and intelligence in animal production, it comes out an increasing need of automatic monitoring of RR in dairy production. This paper introduced six different sensing methods based on chest-abdominal movement and respiratory airflow, either contact or contact free. Aiming to improve the development and practical application of automatic monitoring equipment for physiological parameters of dairy cows, this paper illustrated the research status, advantages and disadvantages, and application prospect of the different sensing methods. It provides a reference for researches on precision and intelligent farming of dairy cow in China.
